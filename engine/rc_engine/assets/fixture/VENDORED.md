# Fixture v3 (revision 3, printed as fixture v5)

Original geometry for this contest: the printed evaluation fixture (grey PLA),
designed in-house, not a downloaded scan.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `fixture_v3.json` | The spec: footprint 250 x 230 mm, 115 mm tall; a low tray (inner 95 x 222 mm, 22 mm walls) on the side facing the robot; a raised block with three vertical holes (Ø28 for M24x70, Ø20 for M16x50, Ø35 for M30x100; 8 mm 45° mouth chamfers; 75/55/105 mm deep) and three hex pockets (41 AF M24, 51 AF M30, 60 AF M36 nut; about half a nut deep; 4 mm 45° lead-ins; flats facing ±y). Millimetres, fixture frame: origin at the footprint centre on the table, +x away from the robot, z up. |
| `fixture_body.stl` | The printed body (binary STL, mm, fixture frame). Visual only in the simulator. Generated by `engine/scripts/build_fixture.py`. |
| `fixture_text.stl` | The REALITY / CHECK inlay on both ±y block faces (0.8 mm, Archivo wdth125 wght900). Visual only. Unchanged since the first v3 print. |
| `source/build_fixture.py` | The original v3 generator (manifold3d, trimesh, shapely and a local `text2d` font helper that is not vendored here, so it does not run from this tree). Kept as the record of how the inlay and the first layout were made; it lays the features out from its own constants. |

## Revision 3 (Nov 2026): a 115 mm block for the dataset bolts, reprint needed

The tasks now use real ISO 4017 bolts (SimReady Hardware, `assets/fasteners/VENDORED.md`): M16x50,
M24x70 and M30x100. The block went from 75 to 115 mm and each hole is 5 mm deeper than its bolt is long:
Ø20 x 55 (M16x50), Ø28 x 75 (M24x70), Ø35 x 105 (M30x100). The hole floors are at 60, 40 and 10 mm, so
10 mm of solid floor stays under the deepest hole (the block is solid down to the table). Hole and pocket
positions, diameters, across-flats, the 8 mm hole chamfers and the 4 mm pocket lead-ins are unchanged;
the hex pockets keep their depths (13 / 15 / 10 mm) from the new top face. The REALITY / CHECK inlay
is unchanged (it sits 23-59 mm up the ±y faces). Webs: unchanged (14 mm or more between mouths, 7 mm to
the block edges). Body volume 3842 cm³ (about 4.8 kg of PLA at 100% infill; print it with the infill you
used before), 4522 faces, watertight after the STL round trip. **The v4 print in the lab is 75 mm tall:
print the new body (`fixture_body.stl` + `fixture_text.stl`).**

The fixture also moved on the table: its origin is now (0.66, 0.15) m from the robot base (was
(0.70, 0.10)), 4 cm toward the robot and 5 cm to +y. At HOME the open gripper's pads hang 91-103 mm above
the table, below the new 115 mm top, and the old position put the block under the right pad. Mark the new
position on the lab table (`fixture.FIXTURE_ORIGIN`; the tray side faces the robot, unrotated).

## Revision 2 (Nov 2026): bigger chamfers, reprint needed

The hole mouth chamfers went from 5 to 8 mm and the hex-pocket chamfers from
2 to 4 mm (both 45°). Hole and pocket positions, diameters, across-flats and
depths are unchanged, so every seat keeps its world position. A hole now
captures a tip landing within its radial clearance + 8 mm of the axis (M16 in
Ø20 about 10 mm). Top-face webs after the change: 14 mm or more between
mouths, 7 mm from the M24 and M30 hole mouths to the block's ±y faces (5 mm of
flat inside the 2 mm edge chamfer), 8 mm from the pocket mouths. **The v3
print in the lab does not have these chamfers: print the new body.** The
spec file keeps its `fixture_v3` name so code and docs paths stay stable.

`engine/scripts/build_fixture.py` rebuilds `fixture_body.stl` from
`fixture_v3.json` alone (plus the vendored inlay, which it cuts out of the
body, as the original did):

```sh
cd engine
uv run --group fixture python scripts/build_fixture.py            # build and check, write nothing
uv run --group fixture python scripts/build_fixture.py --write    # update fixture_body.stl
uv run --group fixture python scripts/build_fixture.py --out DIR  # also copy the print set (body + inlay)
```

It fails unless both meshes are watertight, consistently wound volumes after a
binary STL save and reload with vertex welding (what a slicer reads), the body
and inlay do not overlap, and every mouth keeps 6 mm of top face to the next
mouth and to the block edge. Run on the previous spec (5 mm / 2 mm chamfers) it
reproduces the previous vendored body (same 4526 faces, symmetric difference
under 0.001 mm³), so the script and the original generator agree.
Print: as modelled, base on the bed, no supports; the inlay is a second
material/colour (Bambu X2D, 256 x 256 bed).

## In the simulator

The simulator (`rc_engine/tasks/fixture.py`) reads `fixture_v3.json` for the
seats and builds its own colliders: boxes for the slab, tray walls and the block
(layered by feature depth), and convex prisms around each hole (a 24-gon with a
24-sided 45° chamfer cone) and each hex pocket (the hexagon with a hexagonal
45° lead-in frustum). Only the block's 2 mm edge chamfer is visual only. All
colliders are printed PLA: sliding friction 0.4 with contact priority 1, above
the parts (see docs/simulation-fidelity.md). The STLs are placed at
`fixture.FIXTURE_ORIGIN` with no rotation.
